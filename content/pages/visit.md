## Getting here

We're at 418 Mill Street, a block up from the river. Street parking is free before 10am, and there's a bike rack out front.

## Holding a loaf

Call us before 10am and we'll set a loaf aside with your name on it. We hold orders until 1pm.

## Wholesale and events

We bake for a handful of local restaurants and can do bread for events with a week's notice. Send us an email with the date and headcount.

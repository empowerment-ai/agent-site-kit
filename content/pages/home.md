## Baked this morning

Our country sourdough ferments for thirty-six hours before it ever sees the oven. That slow rise is where the flavor comes from, and it's why the crust crackles when you squeeze it. Friday is juniper levain day. Get here early.

## Pastry worth waking up for

Croissants take us three days from dough to case. We fold cultured butter into every batch by hand, and the cardamom buns are knotted one at a time. They're the reason the line forms at seven.

## Come sit a while

The coffee is roasted a few miles away and the drip is bottomless if you're staying. There are six seats by the window and a long bench outside when the weather cooperates.

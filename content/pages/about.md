## Why "slow"

Most bread you buy was made in about three hours. Ours takes three days if you count the starter feed. Slow fermentation gives the yeast and bacteria time to break the flour down, which means more flavor, a better crust, and bread that keeps longer on your counter.

## Why "Juniper & Rye"

Rye is the flour we reach for most, for its deep, earthy flavor. Juniper is the loaf that started it all: a levain with crushed juniper berries and orange zest that we baked for friends before there was ever a storefront. It's still on the menu every Friday.

## What we won't do

We don't use dough conditioners, bread improvers, or par-baked anything. If it's in the case, it was mixed, shaped, and baked in this building.

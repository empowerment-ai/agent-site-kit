A good sourdough loaf should last you three or four days. Here's how to get there.

## Cut side down

Once you've cut into it, stand the loaf cut-side down on a wooden board. The crust protects everything else. That's all most people need.

## Paper, not plastic

Plastic traps moisture and turns a crackly crust soft and chewy overnight. A paper bag or a linen bread bag lets the crust breathe.

## Freeze it sliced

If you won't finish it in a few days, slice it and freeze it in a zip bag. Toast slices straight from the freezer.

## Reviving a day-old loaf

Run the whole loaf quickly under the tap, then put it in a 375°F oven for about eight minutes. The crust crisps back up and the inside warms through. It's almost like it's fresh from the oven.
